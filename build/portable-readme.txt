HOMECABLE PORTABLE
==================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere — your Documents, another drive, a USB
stick — and double-click HomeCable.exe.

Everything HomeCable keeps (your channels, settings, guide, posters and downloaded
updates) is saved in the "HomeCable Data" folder right here beside it. Move or copy the
whole folder and HomeCable comes with you. It doesn't touch an installed HomeCable on
the same PC (that one keeps its own channels and settings).

Updates: small app updates download and apply by themselves, like the installed
version. When a release needs the full installer instead, download the new portable
zip from https://github.com/OrionVW42/homecable/releases and unzip it over this folder
(your "HomeCable Data" folder stays).

The file portable.txt is what makes this copy portable — leave it here.
By using HomeCable you agree to the terms in license.txt.

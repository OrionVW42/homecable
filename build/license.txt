WHAT HOMECABLE IS
HomeCable is free, open-source software (MIT license) that turns video files

internet streams from public lists. It has no accounts, no tracking and no

FREE LIVE TV & CLASSICS
Optional free stations come from public lists (iptv-org and similar) of
streams that broadcasters themselves make freely available; classic movies
come from the Internet Archive's public-domain collections. Availability and
content are up to each broadcaster. HomeCable hosts none of it.

TORRENTS AND SEARCH
Torrents are off and hidden unless you turn them on in HomeCable and accept a
warning. When on, HomeCable can download and share torrents you add, and search
sources you choose. It comes with one search source, the Internet Archive,
limited to items marked public domain or Creative Commons. It hosts no torrents
and does not supply, list or recommend any other source.

Do not use torrents for anything illegal. Torrents share what you download with
other people and show them your internet address, so always use a VPN. A VPN
does not make anything legal. You alone are responsible for any source you add
and for having the right to download and share what you add. HomeCable and its
authors are not responsible for how you use this feature.

Beta features are new and still being worked on. They are off and hidden unless
you turn them on in HomeCable and accept a warning. They may be wrong or

The Movies tab is a list of films. HomeCable does not host them, and a film
being listed does not mean you have the right to watch, copy or share it. Only
films in your own library and copies the Internet Archive marks as public domain
or Creative Commons can play. Those labels mostly come from whoever uploaded the

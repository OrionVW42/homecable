Why HomeCable exists

Streaming menus turned watching TV into homework. HomeCable brings back the other way: channels that are already playing something, a guide to flip through, and the couch feeling of just watching TV — built out of your own library. You bring the media; HomeCable brings the cable company (minus the bill).

Everything a cable box did — and plenty it didn't

All of it works out of the box after a guided first-run setup that can configure everything for you.

📺

Channels that never stop

Shows start on the half hour, episodes air in order (or shuffled, or fully random — per channel). Channels keep airing while the app is closed: open it next week and the schedule has moved on, exactly on time.

🗓

A real cable guide

A grid guide with what's on now and next across every channel, time blocks ("Saturday Cartoons 8–11am"), and live TV programmes with real titles and times from free public guides.

⏪

Live DVR

Pause, rewind and fast-forward live channels like a cable box — back as far as you've been watching, forward to live.

🎬

On Demand & Stream Demand

A wall of your movie posters with resume, and a binge screen for shows — seasons, episodes, "Continue S2 · E4", and the next episode plays itself.

🎨

Network logos & bumpers

Every channel gets its own network mark — ready-made looks, fonts, shapes, colors, or your own picture. Between shows the channel plays a few seconds of its own animated logo bump, just like real networks.

📣

Wacky commercial breaks

On out of the box (the setup hands you the off switch): short, family-friendly interdimensional joke ads between shows shipped inside the app. They play between your OWN shows only — live TV streams are never touched. Add your own ad packs — a pack is just a folder of short videos, each with its own switch.

🏷

Tags & info, done for you

One click looks everything up by name (titles only): posters, genres, correct names, anime detection with the tags fans sort by, and true airing order so OVAs land between the right seasons.

📱

Watch all over the house

Cast to Chromecast / Google TV (mirror everything or send just the picture). Pair phones and tablets with a QR code to watch anywhere on your network — or use one as the remote, even for the TV.

👪

Profiles & kid-safety

Kids only see kid-friendly channels, teens see more, grown-ups see everything. PIN, password or controller-combo locks — plus a password-locked private section that stays out of every list.

💬

Subtitles & dubs, Netflix-style

Auto-subtitles when the audio isn't your language, per-show audio and subtitle memory, skip-intro for anime, and live captions generated entirely on your own PC.

📡

Free live TV — thousands of stations

Over 10,000 free, publicly available live stations worldwide from the community-run iptv-org index — pick languages, countries and whole regions, no limits. Every station is tested automatically and only ones that play are kept, with a real TV guide. Plus public-domain movies and cartoons from the Internet Archive, scheduled like any other channel.

🎮

Couch-first controls

Keyboard, mouse, or Xbox / PS5 controller for everything — plus a pop-out always-on-top mini player, fullscreen TV mode, and four built-in retro arcade games for the ad breaks of life.

The PC is the heart of the system: it holds your library, builds the schedules, and serves every phone and TV in the house. Phones pair once with a QR code; nothing is ever opened to the internet.

Install in two minutes

  1. Download HomeCable-Setup-<version>.exe from Releases and run it. Agree to the fair-use terms (it won't install without them).
  2. First run: pick Set it all up for me — HomeCable finds your media, sorts movies and shows into channels, adds free TV, and walks you through a short welcome tour.
  3. Phones: Settings → Phone app → pair with the QR code. Updating later? Run the new installer over the old one — channels, people and settings are kept.

Unsigned installer: Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run — choose "More info → Run anyway". Code signing is on the roadmap.

FAQ

Is this for piracy?

No. HomeCable is a player: it hosts and provides no media. It plays files you have the right to play, plus free public streams and public-domain archives. The installer requires agreeing to exactly that, and what you play is your responsibility.

Does it need the internet?

Only for optional features — show info and posters (titles only are sent), free live TV, and TV guides. Your library, viewing and settings never leave your PC. No accounts, no telemetry, no tracking, ever.

What formats does it play?

Basically everything — MP4, MKV, AVI, WMV, MOV, MPEG, TS, FLV, HEVC and more. Anything the player can't decode natively is converted on the fly by the bundled FFmpeg.

Where does my media come from?

This PC, USB drives, network shares, NAS boxes, and DLNA media servers (Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and friends). Drop new files in your folders and they just appear.

How do updates work?

HomeCable updates itself: it checks GitHub Releases, downloads the new version quietly without interrupting what’s playing, and switches over the next time it starts — channels, people and settings are always kept. Settings → Updates sets how often it looks.

Mac or Linux?

Windows-only today. The app is Electron-based, so other desktops are possible later — the phone side already works with any Android device.
